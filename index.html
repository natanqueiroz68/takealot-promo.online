Description

Elevate your kitchen with the Berlinger Haus 15-Piece Cookware Set - a perfect fusion of functionality, design, and modern luxury. Crafted for passionate home chefs and professionals alike, this complete set delivers exceptional performance and timeless elegance.

Exceptional Performance

Each piece is constructed from forged aluminum, a material renowned for its superior heat conductivity and durability. The 3-layer titan Pro non-stick coating ensures flawless food release, effortless cleaning, and long-lasting resistance to scratches and abrasions. Enjoy healthier cooking with little to no oil, while maintaining rich, authentic flavors.

The Turbo Induction Base guarantees rapid and even heat distribution, reducing energy consumption by up to 35%. Designed for maximum versatility, all cookware pieces are suitable for every type of hob, including induction.

Elegant & Thoughtful Design

The ergonomic soft-touch handles and knobs remain cool during use, ensuring a comfortable, secure grip even during long cooking sessions.

The tempered glass lids are equipped with a steam release vent and an innovative soft-close silicone rim system, creating a tight, noise-free seal that locks in heat, moisture, and nutrients for perfectly cooked meals every time.

Smart & Functional Details

Practicality meets luxury with strainer lids that simplify draining and pouring - no need for extra tools. The ECO-friendly construction is PFOA, lead, and cadmium-free, ensuring safe and sustainable cooking for you and your family. The smooth non-stick surface also makes cleaning quick and effortless, saving time after every meal.

Complete 15-Piece Set Includes:

  • Deep frypan Ø24 × 6.5 cm / 2.3 L
  • Frypan Ø28 × 5.2 cm / 2.4 L
  • Casserole with strainer lid Ø20 × 11 cm / 2.6 L
  • Casserole with strainer lid Ø24 × 12.5 cm / 4.3 L
  • Casserole with strainer lid Ø28 × 13 cm / 6.3 L
  • Saucepan with lid Ø16 × 7.5 cm / 1.2 L
  • 3 pcs kitchen tools: slotted turner, spaghetti ladle, soup ladle
  • 2 pcs black silicone mats
